# ZidWix design direction

## Ground-truth reference
The supplied `zidwix website theme.jpeg` is the visual source of truth. Match its page-board architecture: a dark left navigation rail, deep navy hero surfaces, clean white content panels, compact uppercase labels, high-contrast typography, rounded cards, magenta-to-orange accents, and abstract glass/3D forms.

## Design movement
Editorial technology studio / neo-brutalist systems design. The experience should feel like a confident creative-technology partner rather than a generic agency template.

## Core principles
- Make the navigation and page hierarchy feel like a living system.
- Use contrast as the primary visual tool: midnight panels against warm white content areas.
- Keep copy concise, direct, and outcome-focused.
- Use the accent gradient sparingly for action, emphasis, and active states.

## Color philosophy
- Charcoal: #18181B for main website backgrounds and large sections.
- White: #FFFFFF for main content, cards, headings, and clean sections.
- Black: #050505 for navigation, footer, and strong contrast areas.
- Supporting Grey: #71717A for secondary text, borders, icons, and inactive elements.
- Light Grey: #E4E4E7 for card backgrounds, dividers, and subtle sections.

## Layout paradigm
A responsive editorial grid: persistent rail on desktop, top bar on smaller screens, large hero statements, modular card grids, and section transitions from dark to light.

## Signature elements
- ZidWix monogram mark built from a compact Z/X ligature.
- Small numbered eyebrow labels.
- Thin 1px borders and soft corner radii.
- Abstract orbital rings, wireframe cubes, and glass orbs built in CSS.
- Gradient underline and button glow used only on active/primary actions.

## Interaction philosophy
Tactile but restrained: cards lift on hover, navigation highlights the current route, and scroll reveal is subtle. All motion has a reduced-motion fallback.

## Typography system
Use `Space Grotesk` for display and body text, with `DM Mono` for labels, metadata, and navigation numbering. Large display text is tight and sentence-case to echo the reference board.

## Brand voice
Clear, intelligent, energetic, and useful. ZidWix speaks in systems and outcomes, not buzzwords.

## Wordmark and icon
A text wordmark paired with a square monogram: `ZIDWIX` with a slashed X detail. The same mark appears in the header and footer; a standalone SVG favicon/icon keeps the identity recognizable at small sizes.

## Signature brand color
Charcoal #18181B, balanced by white content surfaces and black contrast areas.
